# Student Success Score — Prototype Methodology

The initial score is a transparent 0–100 composite indicator.

- CGPA normalized to 0–100: 25%
- Backlog indicator: 10% (100 minus 22 points per backlog, clamped to 0–100)
- Attendance: 20%
- Assignment completion: 10%
- LMS logins per week, normalized against 7: 5%
- Engagement: 5%
- Aptitude: 8%
- Coding: 7%
- Technical skills: 6%
- Soft skills: 4%

Total = 100%.

Academic risk uses prototype thresholds based on attendance, CGPA, and backlogs. Placement risk uses the average of aptitude, coding, mock interview, technical skills, and soft skills.

These weights and thresholds are initial assumptions for demonstrating the system. They should be reviewed with faculty, tested for sensitivity and fairness, and calibrated against historical outcomes before any predictive claims are made. The composite score is not a probability of success.
